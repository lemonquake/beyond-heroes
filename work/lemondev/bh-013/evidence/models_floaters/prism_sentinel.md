# Prism Sentinel (`prism_sentinel`)
- Script `tools/blender/creatures/build_prism_sentinel.py` -> `game/assets/characters/prism_sentinel.glb` (+ `.glb.import`)
- Triangles: 6996 (core 1306, ring_1 2376, ring_2 2408, ring_3 906). Budget 10k.
- Size: 1.87 m tall (finials +-0.92 m), rings reach ~1.0 m radius. Origin = crystal centre; default 1.2 m hover leaves the lower finial ~0.3 m above ground.
- Nodes (Godot space, front = +Z):
  - `core` (identity): 1.3 m faceted 8-sided double-pyramid crystal, alternating cyan (BH_Aether) / pale-cyan (BH_Ichor) facets, bronze collars + stone prongs + bronze finial spikes top and bottom, and the lens-eye (bronze bezel, dark iris ring, blazing white-cyan BH_Emissive lens) centred at about (0, 0, +0.40) Godot = the beam origin, facing the model's front like ice_wraith's face.
  - `ring_1` rot X70 deg: bronze gyroscope band r 0.76 m, 4 stone clamps with cyan prism shards, 12 bronze studs.
  - `ring_2` authored tilt X18/Y52 deg: bronze band r 0.86 m, 5 clamps + shards.
  - `ring_3` rot X-10 deg: 3 orbiting lens crystals with bronze rims at r 1.0 m.
- Palette (`__prism_sentinel`): BH_Bronze (0.6,0.38,0.16 metal 0.9), BH_Stone weathered (0.5,0.48,0.44), BH_Aether cyan crystal (emit 0.45,0.88,1.0 x3), BH_Ichor pale crystal (emit 0.7,0.95,1.0 x1.5), BH_Emissive lens (0.8,1.0,1.0 x12), BH_Shadow iris.
- Distinct from star_mote (small violet star in a brass orrery with planets): big cyan crystal body, front eye, stone clamps and prism shards; much larger.
- Limitations: rings clear the core's spikes at their authored radii; with the game's pulse (core up to 1.35x while rings shrink to 0.75x) the finials can briefly pass through ring_1.
- Validation: scratch Godot 4.7.2 import (`tools/godot_check_floaters.py`, log `logs/godot_check.txt`): loads, 0 import errors, no skeleton / AnimationPlayer, all nodes and `BH_*__prism_sentinel` materials present. Build log: `logs/build_prism_sentinel.log`.
- Evidence: `prism_sentinel_rest_iso.png` (gameplay camera 54 deg at 16 / 22 m 1:1 crops, back view, 2x zooms, 7 m), `prism_sentinel_views.png` (4 side views, from-above view, close-up). Workbench renders: emissive materials show as flat bright colour (no bloom).
- Pipeline: `"<blender>" -b --factory-startup --python tools/blender/creatures/build_prism_sentinel.py -- [--preview DIR]`; shared helpers in `tools/blender/creatures/kit_d13_float.py`. No clips (static; the game animates the nodes, see `character_visual.gd` `_animate_floating`).
