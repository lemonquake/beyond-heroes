# rune_golem: Rune Golem (bh-010, Builder B)

- Module: `tools/blender/characters/enemy_rune_golem.py`. GLB: `game/assets/characters/rune_golem.glb` (3.1 MB, 47 clips, clip lengths verified)
- Height: 2.93 m rest, 2.91 m idle (T-pose span 4.42 m)
- Tris: 12,692 (6,736 verts, 191 parts)
- Attack clips: gs_1, boss_slam, cast_heavy, boss_sweep, boss_charge (+ base set)
- Palette (`rune_golem`): dark slate slabs (Stone), bronze bands, clamps, pauldron fronts, fist straps and toe caps (Bronze), basalt core column and joint balls (DarkSteel, non-metal), carved grooves (Shadow, emission removed). BH_Emissive has neutral white base (0.92, 0.92, 0.9) and emission (0.95, 0.95, 0.92) x6.
- BH_Emissive contract: used only by the rune channel inlays (chest, back, abdomen, pelvis, tassets, pauldron tops, upper arms, forearms, fists, knees, shins, thighs, forehead), the chest rune core (0.15 m sphere in a bronze-clamped socket) and the eye slits. No other material has emission in the GLB (checked in the GLB JSON: `BH_Emissive__rune_golem` is the only material with an emissiveFactor). Every channel is a dark BH_Shadow groove with the glowing inlay just proud of it. rune_golem_tint.png recolours only BH_Emissive to fire, frost, storm and poison.
- Build: stacked, overlapping slab blocks (pelvis on hips, abdomen on spine, chest on chest) around a basalt core column that is blended hips > spine > chest, so no gaps open. Huge squared chest, pectoral slabs, tilted slab pauldrons on the clavicle bones (they stay put when the arms swing), a small head-stone sunk between the pauldrons with a brow slab and a cap stone. Massive forearms, block fists with knuckle stones and a thumb block, short pillar legs, broad slab feet.
- Distinct from the Aether Sentinel: solid, not floating, segments; no maul, shield or visor; much heavier and squarer at 2.93 m vs 2.0 m; dark slate with white (tintable) runes vs weathered stone with cyan Aether. See the lineup tiles in rune_golem_rest_iso.png.
- Deformation (rune_golem_audit.txt): near-rigid. Worst stretch 1.26x, dL 11 cm, only on the hidden core column.
- Evidence: rune_golem_rest_iso.png, rune_golem_head_closeup.png, rune_golem_clips.png (idle, 5 attacks, hit_heavy, death_back), rune_golem_tint.png, rune_golem_audit.txt
- Limitations: the segments are rigid per bone, so at extreme bends the slab edges overlap (intended construct look). The fists dip up to 16 cm below z=0 at the boss_slam impact, and gs_1 dips 7 cm. The rune channels are about 4.4 cm wide; at the 24 m camera they read as thin lines, and the core and pauldron-top runes carry the read.
