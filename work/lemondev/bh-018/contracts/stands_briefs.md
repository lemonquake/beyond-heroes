# bh-018 stand briefs (W × D footprint in metres; front = -Y)

## Builder A — `assets_market_a.py`
1. **stand_provisions** — Tovin, Provisioner of Malasugue (health/mana draughts, scrolls, bombs, supplies). 4.4 × 3.0.
   Sturdy timber stall: plank counter across the front with crates of corked red and blue draught bottles (BH_GemRed /
   BH_GemAqua liquid, BH_Bottle glass), rolled scrolls in a basket, grain sacks, two barrels and a small powder keg at one
   end; back shelves with more bottles. A single-colour green canvas (BH_ClothGreen) lean-to awning propped forward on
   two poles, **rising toward the front** (front edge ~3.0 m, back ~2.4 m), bundles of drying herbs and a string of
   garlic hanging from the front beam. npc behind the counter, left of centre.
2. **stand_arcana** — Seris, Aether Mystic (rings, charms, staves, mana draughts). Ø 4.2 hexagonal pavilion: deep violet
   (BH_ClothViolet) six-sided tent roof on carved posts, gold finial with a crescent-and-star ornament, the two front
   panels tied back as an entrance; inside a round cloth-draped table with a crystal ball (BH_GemViolet glow), star-chart
   scroll, candles; an open casket of rings and amulets glinting; a rack of staves; hanging crystal chimes. npc at the
   entrance so she is visible.
3. **stand_lapidary** — Ysolde Marr, Lapidary — the **Socket Specialist** of Malasugue (adds and removes sockets, sets
   and cuts crystals, sells crystals). 3.8 × 3.0. Stone-footed timber booth with a copper-sheathed roof gone green
   (BH_Verdigris) — a steep single pitch; a treadle-driven gem-cutting wheel at one end of the counter, a big brass
   magnifying lens on an articulated arm, a glass-fronted display cabinet of glowing crystals in six colours
   (GemAmber, GemAqua, GemGold, GemViolet, GemGreen, GemRed), geode halves and crystal clusters on the counter, tongs,
   chisels, a small anvil block. The glow of the stones is the stand's identity.
4. **stand_smithy** — Brannoc, Blacksmith of Malasugue (weapons, armour, shields) **and the town Forge station**.
   5.6 × 4.2. Open-fronted timber-framed smithy lean-to with a dark slate roof (BH_Slate) and a stone chimney at the
   back right above a forge hearth with glowing coals (BH_Coals), leather bellows, anvil on a stump front-right, quench
   barrel, a weapon rack with swords/axes/spears on the left, shields hung on the back wall, armour on a stand, tongs and
   hammers on a board. Sockets: npc by the anvil, `use` in front of the anvil, `flame` at the coals, `smoke` at the
   chimney top.
5. **stand_wagon** — The Hooded Stranger, dealer in rare wares. 4.8 × 2.4 (shafts included). A dark, weathered
   travelling merchant's covered wagon: black hoop-top cover (BH_ClothBlack) over ribs, big spoked wheels, the side hatch
   folded down on chains as a counter facing -Y showing curios (ornate locked caskets, a skull, faintly glowing odd
   bottles, a sealed scroll, a small idol), a lantern on a hook, shafts resting on a wooden block (no horse). npc beside
   the hatch.

   (BH_Velvet) of rings and amulets, a scalloped teal canopy (BH_ClothTeal) with gold fringe on a carved frame, a brass
   balance scale, a jewellery tree hung with necklaces, a carved crane (the bird) finial on top.

   with a thatched roof overhang (BH_Thatch), shelves of jars and bottles of many colours, bundles of herbs drying from
   a rack of poles, mortar and pestle, a small copper alembic distilling over a tiny brazier, baskets of herbs on the
   counter front.
8. **stand_armsbroker** — Corvin Ashby, Arms Broker of Olivar (premium weapons and armour). 5.0 × 3.4. Prosperous
   broker's pavilion: sturdy timber frame, heavy crimson canopy (BH_ClothRed) with black scalloped valance, a polished
   plate armour mannequin, a tall wall rack of premium weapons (greatsword, spear, axe, bow), shields with painted
   heraldic devices (shapes only, no letters), a heavy ledger desk with an iron strongbox.
9. **stand_gemcutter** — Anselm Cray, Gem-cutter — the **Socket Specialist** of Olivar. 3.8 × 3.8. Must look nothing
   like the lapidary: an octagonal open-sided kiosk with a steep pointed lead-grey roof (BH_Slate or BH_Iron) crowned by a
   large glowing crystal finial, a round central workbench with a foot-powered cutting wheel, crystal specimens under
   glass bell jars around the rim, a rack of fine tools. npc inside by the bench but visible through the open front.
10. **stand_crystal_cart** — Dagna Flint, Crystal Prospector — the **Socket Specialist** of Wyman Outpost (a rough hero
    camp). 3.6 × 2.6. A miner's two-wheeled handcart heaped with raw crystal ore and geodes glowing faintly in several
    colours, a pick-axe and shovel leaning on it, an ochre canvas shade (BH_ClothOchre) on two poles, a folding table
    with a scale and a crystal-splitting block, a lantern.
