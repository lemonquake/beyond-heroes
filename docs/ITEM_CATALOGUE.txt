11. SOCKET CRYSTALS AND THE SOCKET SPECIALISTS (BH-018)
======================================================================================================================
  Every piece of equipment can have sockets opened by a Socket Specialist: Ysolde Marr (Marr's Lapidary, Malasugue),
  Anselm Cray (Cray's Cutting Room, Olivar) and Dagna Flint (Flint's Crystal Cart, Wyman Outpost). The most sockets
  a piece can hold depends on its tier (section 1): from 1 (Beginner, Common) to 7 (Aether).

Services
--------
  Add Socket — opens one more socket. Fee = 400 x (sockets after opening)^2 x (1 + item level / 20).
  Remove Socket — closes an empty socket. Fee = 200 x open sockets x (1 + item level / 20).
  Set Crystal — free. A set crystal stays until the piece is purged or crystallized.
  Purge — breaks every crystal out of the piece: the crystals are destroyed, the piece keeps its (now empty)
  sockets. Fee = 300 x crystals x (1 + item level / 20).
  Crystallization — destroys the piece; every crystal set in it returns to the bag. Fee = 10% of the crystals' shop
  value (at least 100).

  Example Add Socket fees for an item level 20 piece: socket 1: 800, socket 2: 3,200, socket 3: 7,200, socket 4: 12,800, socket 5: 20,000, socket 6: 28,800, socket 7: 39,200

Where crystals come from
------------------------
  Every boss always drops one crystal of any grade (higher grades grow likelier with the boss's level). Every
  miniboss (champion) always drops a Fragment or a Shard. Aetherift is rare: 4% of boss crystals, 1% of champion
  crystals. The three Specialists sell every family except Aetherift from 5,000 gold a Fragment (Shards,
  Crystallines and Orbitals unlock with hero level); Aetherift Fragments are sold at a higher level only.

12. WEAPON UPGRADES: ENCHANTMENT AND FORE-TECH
======================================================================================================================
  Enchantment (at an Alchemy Table): a rune, rank I-III, turns a share of the weapon's damage into an element and
  adds elemental gifts. Fore-Tech (at a Forge): a mechanical refit +1..+5, +2% weapon damage per rank plus its own
  bonus. Both can be on one weapon; a different rune or refit starts over at rank 1.

Enchantments
------------
  Flame Etching — A rune of cinders. Part of every hit burns, and Burning hurts more.
      I: 20% Fire; +4 Added Fire Damage; 10% increased Burning Damage
      II: 30% Fire; +8 Added Fire Damage; 20% increased Burning Damage
      III: 40% Fire; +13 Added Fire Damage; 32% increased Burning Damage
  Frost Etching — A rune of rime. Part of every hit is ice, and Chill builds toward Freeze faster.
      I: 20% Ice; +4 Added Ice Damage; 10% increased Status Chance
      II: 30% Ice; +8 Added Ice Damage; 18% increased Status Chance
      III: 40% Ice; +13 Added Ice Damage; 28% increased Status Chance
  Storm Etching — A rune of thunder. Part of every hit is lightning, and the weapon moves quicker.
      I: 20% Lightning; +4 Added Lightning Damage; 3% increased Attack Speed
      II: 30% Lightning; +8 Added Lightning Damage; 6% increased Attack Speed
      III: 40% Lightning; +13 Added Lightning Damage; 9% increased Attack Speed
  Stone Etching — A rune of the deep. Part of every hit is earth; armor gives way and poise breaks sooner.
      I: 20% Earth; +5% Armor Penetration; 10% increased Stagger Strength
      II: 30% Earth; +9% Armor Penetration; 20% increased Stagger Strength
      III: 40% Earth; +14% Armor Penetration; 30% increased Stagger Strength
  Radiant Etching — A rune of dawn. Part of every hit is light, and healing you give or receive is stronger.
      I: 20% Light; +4 Added Light Damage; 6% increased Healing Effectiveness
      II: 30% Light; +8 Added Light Damage; 12% increased Healing Effectiveness
      III: 40% Light; +13 Added Light Damage; 20% increased Healing Effectiveness
  Umbral Etching — A rune of dusk. Part of every hit is shadow, and a share of the damage returns as life.
      I: 20% Dark; +4 Added Dark Damage; +1.5% Life Leech
      II: 30% Dark; +8 Added Dark Damage; +3% Life Leech
      III: 40% Dark; +13 Added Dark Damage; +5% Life Leech

Fore-Tech refits
----------------
  Whetted Edge (best on any weapon) — Grind and re-hone: more Physical Damage. Per rank: 6% increased Physical
  Damage
  Balanced Grip (best on fast weapons) — Rebalance the weight: swings come faster and find weak spots. Per rank: 3%
  increased Attack Speed; +0.5% Critical Chance
  Serrated Teeth (best on daggers, claws, axes) — Cut a row of teeth: critical hits hit harder and bleeding bites
  deeper. Per rank: +8% Critical Damage; 6% increased Damage over Time
  Weighted Head (best on hammers, great axes, clubs) — Cast in a heavy cap: bigger stagger and harder collisions.
  Per rank: 8% increased Stagger Strength; 10% increased Impact Damage
  Piercing Point (best on spears, swords, arrows) — Narrow the tip: ignores part of the target's armor. Per rank:
  +4% Armor Penetration
  Fine Sights (best on bows and javelins) — Fit a sighting notch and true the shaft: straighter, faster, deadlier
  shots. Per rank: +1.2% Critical Chance; 4% increased Projectile Damage; 5% increased Projectile Speed
