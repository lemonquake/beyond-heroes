## Stats

Hold + to add points repeatedly. All assigns every remaining point to that attribute. Apply Points commits your choices; Clear discards them.

## Party chat

Press Enter or tap Chat to write a message. Multiplayer keeps a scrollable party chat log at the side; messages also appear above heroes for 3 seconds. Type @ and a player name, then press Right, Space or Enter to complete the suggested name (or tap a suggestion). Press Enter again to send. Names containing spaces are completed as @"Player Name". Use @everyone to ping the whole party. Mentioned players hear an alert and see the sender's message flash. Use the Emoji button to insert an emoji. Both devices need this game version.

## Belt keys

Q and E use the first two belt keys. Alt+Q, Alt+W, Alt+E and Alt+R are four more quick keys, empty until you put a potion or scroll on them: click a key slot at the bottom of the Inventory (or on the HUD), drag a consumable onto it, or hover one in the bag and press the key. The "Key:" button under each slot (or Settings > Controls) changes the key; combinations like Ctrl+1 work too.

## First-person view

Press V (or Settings > Gameplay > First-Person View). The mouse looks around, WASD moves where you look, attacks and spells go to the crosshair. Open any window and the cursor is free again. On a touch screen, drag on open ground to look around.
