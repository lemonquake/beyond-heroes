# Knockback and side-ability limits

Knockback speed is capped at 12 m/s after damage, weight, critical-hit and equipment modifiers. A push can move a target at most 3 m horizontally and lasts at most 0.75 seconds. Another hit can replace a weaker push but cannot add momentum, renew the travel budget or extend the duration. Afterward, the target has 0.6 seconds of protection from another push or throw; damage and other status effects still apply.

Throws are capped at 6 m/s upward speed after weight scaling and at 1 m upward travel. Airborne targets cannot be relaunched. Instant Gravity Pull movement is limited to 3 m per cast and still checks walls and resistance. Wall and body impact damage is capped at 20% of maximum HP per collision; normal body momentum transfer is reduced to 40%.

Poise breaks disable a target for at most 0.75 seconds. Further hits cannot refresh that stagger or break poise again during its 0.6-second recovery window. Full status resistance prevents stagger instead of creating a status with an infinite duration.

One-time side upgrades, major talents and keystones have a maximum level of 1. Repeatable side upgrades have a maximum level of 4. Main skills, passive skills and minor talents retain their maximum level of 25. Side-upgrade effects obey their caps even when given an invalid rank.

Shield Bash knockback is reduced from 17 to 8 m/s. Battering Ram adds 1 m/s knockback and 1 m charge distance per effective rank. Leap Slam uses 8 m/s knockback and 4 m/s launch speed. Skybreaker adds 0.5 m/s launch and 0.3 m radius per effective rank. Punch Through adds 1 m/s knockback. Cluster Charges adds 1 m/s launch and 0.6 m radius. Resolved skill parameters and multiplayer hit results also enforce the global force caps.

Loading an older save clamps affected ranks and returns the points spent above the new caps to the corresponding skill or talent pool. Saving the migrated hero prevents a second refund.

## Gem upgrades

At any Forge, Alchemy Table or Workbench, open Recipes and select a recipe under Gem Upgrades. Four loose gems of the same family and tier make one gem of the next tier: Fragment → Shard → Crystalline → Orbital. All twelve families are supported, including celestial gems. Orbital is the maximum tier. Upgrades require no gold, recipe scroll or extra hero levels and support batches. Locked, favorite and socketed gems are kept. Crafting checks output space before consuming inputs.
