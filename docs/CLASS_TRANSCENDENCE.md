# Class Transcendence

Every hero can advance their class twice: once at level 60 and once at level 120. Each starting class has one first
advancement and two master classes to choose between, sixteen classes in all.

| Starting class | Level 60 | Level 120 (choose one) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knight | Royal Guard | Dark General or Grand Paladin |
| Hunter | Tracker | Wildwarden or Starstrider |
| Mage | Arcanist | Archmage or Void Sovereign |
| Shadowblade | Nightstalker | Phantom Reaper or Blood Sovereign |

The Hunter was called Ranger before; saved heroes keep working unchanged.

## How to advance

- Visit **Grand Master Edran Vale** in any Guild House. You do not need to join a guild, pay, or finish a quest.
- Reaching level 60 or 120 only makes the advancement available; nothing is chosen for you. The Character window shows
  a reminder until you advance.
- A hero who is already level 121 or higher can take both advancements in one visit.
- You keep your level, experience, attributes, points, skills, talents, gear, guild, quests, companions and appearance.
- The master class choice is permanent. The window shows both choices side by side, then asks you to review and
  confirm. Resetting skills or talents does not change it.

## What each advancement gives

- **Three new skills** at level 1. They go into empty skill bar slots; if the bar is full, assign them from Skills.
- **Three new talents** at level 1, on their own page in Talents.
- **A signature trait**, always on (see below). A master keeps the trait of its first advancement.
- **Class gear**: a weapon, an armour and an accessory for the class, sold by the Grand Master's armory (Elite rarity)
  and found as loot from level 60 (first advancement) or 120 (master classes).

The granted skill and talent levels are free: they never cost points and are never refunded. Further levels use the
usual skill and talent points.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Grand Paladin | Dawnbringer | Every 5th weapon hit releases light around you: 60% weapon damage as Light to enemies and 2% of Maximum HP to you and allies nearby (once every 3 s). |
| Tracker | Hunter's Opening | The first arrow, bolt or javelin to hit an enemy at full health is a critical hit and deals 25% more damage. |
| Wildwarden | Rooted Stance | After standing still for 1 s in a fight: +10% Defense, +30% Knockback Resistance, and your shots slow their targets. Moving ends it. |
| Starstrider | Star Chart | Hits on enemies more than 10 m away add a star (up to 5). With 5, the next shot is a Shooting Star: 60% more damage and it pierces every enemy in line. |

| Archmage | Elemental Attunement | Damaging spells cycle Fire, Ice and Lightning and send an extra bolt of the current element (20% of the hit) at the first enemy struck. |
| Void Sovereign | Collapse | An enemy defeated by your spell pulls nearby enemies toward it and deals 30% of the final hit to them as Dark damage (once every 2 s). |
| Nightstalker | From the Shadows | After a dodge, the next weapon attack within 2 s deals 25% more damage and adds 1 Combo. |
| Phantom Reaper | Soul Harvest | Each enemy you defeat gives 1 Combo and takes 2 s off Phantom Crossing's cooldown (once per second). |
| Blood Sovereign | Blood Price | An enemy that dies while Bleeding spreads 40% of its bleed to enemies within 3 m and heals you 2% of Maximum HP (once per second). |

## Who can wear what

Every piece of equipment says which classes may wear it, and the rule is enforced:

- **For any class**: most ordinary gear.
- **For Knight class**: a starting class and every class it advances into.
- **For Royal Guard and its master classes**: first-advancement gear.
- **For Grand Paladin only**: master gear.

Gear you cannot wear can still be sold, stored or traded. Unbound gear skips level and attribute requirements but not
the class rule. Companions (Tempos) keep their own equipment rules.

## Other players

Other players see your current class in the line under your name (for example "Level 121 Grand Paladin · PC"), in the
class's colour, and see your class armour when you wear it. On the official server the new class shows once the
server has confirmed the save.
