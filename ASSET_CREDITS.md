# Asset credits — Beyond Heroes

body the project author supplied, and the CC0 downloads listed below: Poly Haven props and Quaternius creatures (bh-033,
`docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md`) and Kenney environment models (map-design pass, `ASSET_CREDITS_MAP_DESIGN.md`).
